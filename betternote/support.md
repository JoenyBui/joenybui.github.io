# BetterNote Support

## Common questions

**My notes aren't syncing.**
Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is turned on for BetterNote in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Then open BetterNote's Settings and tap **Sync Now**.

**How do I move from Evernote?**
In Evernote, export a notebook as an `.enex` file. Then, in BetterNote, open Settings > **Import .enex File…**.

**How do I share a notebook?**
Right-click or long-press the notebook and choose **Share Notebook…**. The people you invite need an Apple ID.

**Can I get my notes out?**
Yes. Settings > **Export All Notes as Markdown…** writes each note to a plain `.md` file.
